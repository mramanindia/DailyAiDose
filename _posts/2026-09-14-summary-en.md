## [Language Is an Insufficient Substrate for Quantitative Reasoning, and Consequential Domains Need Large Quantitative Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12105) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A new paper argues that language models are fundamentally unsuitable for consequential quantitative decisions like risk pricing or capital allocation because natural language descriptions lose irreversible information from raw data, and LLMs cannot provide the reproducibility, lineage, and calibrated uncertainty that such domains demand.
